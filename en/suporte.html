Frequently asked questions

Is my data safe?
Yes. It stays in your private iCloud, encrypted and tied to your Apple Account. Not even Hermes Digital has access to it.

Do I need to create an account?
No. The app does not require sign-up, an email, or a password.

How does automatic lab report reading work?
With your permission, the file is sent through our server to Gemini, Google’s artificial intelligence service, which returns the structured data (see the Privacy Policy). You review everything before saving.

How do I switch to a new device?
Just sign in with the same Apple Account; with iCloud turned on, your data syncs automatically.

How do I delete my data?
You can delete any record within the app, or remove the app and its data from iCloud in your iPhone’s Settings.
