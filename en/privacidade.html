VITAFOLIO

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Vitafolio is a personal and family health record for iPhone. This policy explains, in plain language, how your data is handled. The core principle is simple: your health data stays in your private iCloud, under your control. Signing in with Sign in with Apple is optional and serves only to keep your data continuous and to enable premium features — never to store your health history.

1. Who we are

Vitafolio is developed by Hermes Digital. App identifier: app.hermesdigital.vitafolio. Contact: hermesdigitalbr@gmail.com.

2. Where your data is stored

All the information you record — profiles, lab reports, markers, medications, vaccines, appointments, documents, vitals, symptoms, cycle, pregnancy — is stored locally on your device and, when you use iCloud, in your iCloud private database (CloudKit), encrypted and tied to your Apple ID.

We do not keep servers with your health history, and we do not sell or share your data. The only exception is AI reading of lab reports, which you choose to use and which is described in section 4.

3. Sign in with Apple

You can use Vitafolio without signing in. If you wish, you can sign in with Sign in with Apple. We do not create a password and do not ask for a traditional sign-up. We receive only an anonymous identifier provided by Apple and, if you allow it, a contact email — which may be Apple’s own private relay email, without revealing your real address.

This sign-in serves only to recognize that it is you (so your data is not lost when you switch devices, and to unlock the premium subscription). It does not store your health history: lab reports, medications, documents, and other records remain in your private iCloud (CloudKit), as described in section 2.

4. AI extraction of lab reports

When you choose to have a lab report (PDF or photo) read automatically, the app asks for your permission before the first upload. With your permission, the file is sent through our server to Gemini, Google’s artificial intelligence service, solely to turn the document into structured data (markers, values, ranges, and date). If you prefer, you can decline and enter the values by hand.

  • Our server does not keep the file: it is passed on to Google and discarded after reading.
  • No health data is stored by us.
  • We use the paid Gemini API: under Google’s terms, the content is not used to train its models. Google may keep logs for a limited time solely to detect abuse, as set out in the Gemini API terms.
  • You always review and confirm what was read before any data enters the app.

To manage fair use of the service, we keep only a quota counter associated with an anonymous identifier derived from your iCloud account — without any health information.

5. Device permissions

  • Camera — only to scan the lab reports and documents you choose.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — only to lock access to the app on your device.
  • Notifications — only for dose reminders and to-dos that you set up. With the Face ID lock turned on, notifications and widgets do not show the name of the medication or the appointment.
  • Apple Health — only if you turn on sending in Vitals: the weight, blood pressure, and blood glucose you record in Vitafolio are written to the Health app. Vitafolio does not read data from the Health app.

The app does not collect location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

6. Sharing

Any sharing of a document (for example, sending a PDF through another app) starts with an action of yours, through iOS’s own share menu. We do not act as an intermediary and do not receive a copy.

7. Your rights (LGPD)

Because the data stays under your control on your device and iCloud, you can at any time edit or delete any record within the app, or remove the app and your data. For questions about data processing, write to hermesdigitalbr@gmail.com.

8. Children and family members

The app lets you create profiles for family members (including children) you care for. These profiles are created and managed by you, in your private iCloud, and are covered by the same protections in this policy.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top indicates the version currently in effect.

Vitafolio organizes health information and never replaces the guidance of a health professional. See the Medical Disclaimer.
